an already-extracted knowledge graph (concepts + relationships). You will be given
the document title and a compact list of the highest-signal concepts and relations.

Write a JSON object, and NOTHING else, with this shape:

{
  "thesis": "<one sentence: the book's central argument>",
  "core_concepts": ["<5-8 concept names, most central first>"],
  "key_principles": ["<up to 3 actionable principles the book argues for>"],
  "audience": "<one sentence: who should read this and why>",
  "summary": "<~150 words synthesising the above into prose>"
}

Ground every statement in the supplied concepts/relations — do not introduce
facts that are not represented in the graph.
